Client Guidance

Frequently Answered Questions

Essential insights regarding foreign ownership regulations, escrow security, and our turnkey discovery protocol.

Can non-resident international buyers hold 100% freehold title? ▼
Yes. All Azure Bay residences and land parcels convey with unencumbered freehold certificates of title issued through accredited sovereign notaries. Our legal advisory team structures cross-border ownership through direct personal holding or specialized private offshore entities (SPVs) tailored to your tax residency.
How does the VIP Discovery Stay program work? ▼
Qualified prospective buyers are welcomed as our guests for a 2-night stay in an oceanfront villa suite. The program includes private airport transfers, a yacht tour of the coastal reserve, architectural material reviews with lead partners, and curated dining. Should you acquire a residence, all associated discovery trip expenses are fully credited at closing.
How are reservation deposits and financial transactions safeguarded? ▼
All reservation deposits are held strictly in independent tier-1 bank escrow accounts governed by international notary custody. Funds are 100% refundable during your statutory due diligence cooling-off window (14 to 30 days depending on contract type).
Do you provide in-house asset management and rental yields? ▼
Yes. Our dedicated private estate concierge offers turnkey property stewardship: 24/7 groundskeeping, security, biometric access maintenance, and optional entry into our luxury short-term rental collection. Participating owners historically achieve 8.2% to 11.4% net annualized yields with zero operational burden.
